Every second, one leaf turns from green to pink. Every minute, a small bird flies in, and each bird has a different color. When the next bird arrives and lands on the tree, the previous bird flies away. At the top of every hour, the canopy bursts apart, scattering the pink leaves across the screen. Then it reassembles，each leaf travels back along its original path and turns green again. The tree stays empty for that first minute, and the first new bird arrives at minute one.
