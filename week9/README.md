## Pink Bird

The bird uses the same design as the one in my clock. The difference is that this time it is controlled by my hand gestures and my face, using two ml5.js models (handPose and faceMesh). When there is no hand in the camera view, the bird lands on top of my head; when my hand appears, it flies over and perches on my index finger.
